Peiqi Yu is a **third-year Ph.D. candidate** in the [Intelligent Control Lab](https://icontrol.ri.cmu.edu/) at the [Department of ECE](https://www.ece.cmu.edu/) of [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/), advised by [Prof. Changliu Liu](https://icontrol.ri.cmu.edu/people/changliu.html).

<div class="research-question" role="note" aria-label="Central research question">
  <span class="research-question-label">Central research question</span>
  <p>How can a robot organize and improve its own capabilities so that its autonomy grows with experience?</p>
</div>

Specifically, I am interested in solving **long-horizon manipulation** problems. My current research interests are in **agentic robotics**, **in-context learning**, and **skill-based systems**. In the future, I am also interested in extending this work to **loco-manipulation** and **dexterous manipulation**.

<div class="research-topics" aria-label="Research interests">
  <span>Agentic robotics</span>
  <span>In-context learning</span>
  <span>Skill-based systems</span>
  <span>Long-horizon manipulation</span>
</div>

I received my **B.Eng. in Automation** from [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/) in June 2023. At Tsinghua, I worked with [Prof. Jiwen Lu](https://ivg.au.tsinghua.edu.cn/Jiwen_Lu/) on computer vision.
